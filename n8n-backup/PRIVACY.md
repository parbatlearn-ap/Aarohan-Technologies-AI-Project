# Privacy Policy — Aarohan OnboardAI (demo)

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Aarohan OnboardAI is a personal portfolio and course capstone project built by Amol Parbat. "Aarohan Technologies" is a fictional company, and all employee records in the app are made-up demo data.

## What the app accesses

The app connects to one Google account, the developer's own account, through Google sign-in. It uses that account only to:

- **Gmail:** send welcome emails to demo new hires.
- **Google Calendar:** create onboarding orientation events.

The app does not read, store or analyse anyone's inbox or calendar contents.

## What data is stored

- Demo employee, task and policy records are stored in a Supabase database.
- Workflow run logs are stored on the project's n8n server and deleted automatically after 3 days.

## Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. Policy questions may be sent to AI providers (Groq and Google Gemini) to generate answers. These contain only fictional demo content.
