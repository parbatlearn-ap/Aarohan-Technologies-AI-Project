How this works

Every AI answer is produced through tools, not guessed: the browser calls a secure Netlify Function, which calls Groq (LLM), Supabase (new-hire data) and four custom n8n MCP servers (Policy Lookup, Policy Intelligence, Onboarding Progress, New Hire Welcome → Gmail + Google Calendar). Results show which MCP tool answered.

How to welcome a new hire

  1. Select the new hire from the list (or add a new one with + Add a new hire). You'll see straight away whether their welcome email has already been sent.
  2. Generate the welcome message. AI writes a personal note based on their role, department, joining date and work mode.
  3. Review and edit the message in the box. You can change any wording before sending.
  4. Send. The hire gets a branded welcome email. For virtual roles, an orientation meeting is also added to Google Calendar.

What OnboardAI does

The time between a candidate accepting an offer and their first day is where most onboarding problems happen: missing documents, delayed background checks, unanswered policy questions and new hires who feel ignored. OnboardAI gives People Ops one place to welcome new hires, answer their policy questions and track their onboarding, with AI doing the repetitive work.

The onboarding journey in the app

  1. Offer accepted → add the new hire. New Hire Welcome tab → + Add a new hire. A standard checklist of onboarding tasks is created automatically.
  2. Welcome them. Select the hire → Generate welcome message → review and edit → Send. They get a branded email, and virtual hires also get a calendar invite for orientation.
  3. Answer their questions. Policy Assistant tab: look up an official policy, ask a question in plain English, or chat with the AI assistant.
  4. Track progress. Onboarding Progress tab: completion %, overdue tasks, blockers (for example a background-check problem) and what to do next.
  5. See everyone at a glance. Dashboard: all new hires, their progress and whether they've been welcomed.

Each tab in one line

  • Dashboard: summary numbers and the list of new hires.
  • New Hire Welcome: add hires, draft and send welcome emails.
  • Policy Assistant: Quick Lookup shows the exact policy text and source document. Policy Intelligence searches all policies and says how confident it is. The chat answers questions and shows which tool it used.
  • Onboarding Progress: one hire's full status, blockers and next steps.

Built-in safety rules

  • Questions about pay, legal matters, grievances or termination are never answered by AI. They are passed to People Ops.
  • Policy answers come only from Aarohan's policy documents, and the source is shown. If confidence is low, the app says so and suggests human review.
  • Every result shows which tool produced it, so nothing is a hidden guess.

What runs behind the scenes

Browser → secure server function (Netlify) → AI model (Groq) for writing and chat · onboarding database (Supabase) for hires and tasks · four automation tools on n8n (MCP servers): Policy Lookup, Policy Intelligence, Onboarding Progress and New Hire Welcome (Gmail + Google Calendar).

Try it in 3 minutes

  1. Dashboard: see the new hires and their progress.
  2. New Hire Welcome: pick EMP-2001 Devika Rao → Generate → Send (it will say she was already welcomed).
  3. Policy Assistant: click the Leave policy chip, then the Onboarding Day-1 chip.
  4. Onboarding Progress: pick EMP-1003 Arjun Verma, a blocked hire with a background-check issue.
  5. Chat: ask “How is EMP-1003 doing?”, then “Can I get a salary hike?” to see the escalation rule.
