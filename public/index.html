How this works

Every AI answer is produced through tools, not guessed: the browser calls a secure Netlify Function, which calls Groq (LLM), Supabase (new-hire data) and four custom n8n MCP servers (Policy Lookup, Policy Intelligence, Onboarding Progress, New Hire Welcome → Gmail + Google Calendar). Pick a new hire and generate a message. Groq drafts a personalised note; sending runs the New Hire Welcome MCP, which emails the hire via Gmail and — for virtual roles — creates a Google Calendar orientation invite.

Ask a policy question

Quick Lookup = exact policy record (MCP: Policy Lookup). Policy Intelligence = semantic search over policy chunks in Supabase pgvector with confidence scoring (MCP: Policy Intelligence), summarised by Groq.
